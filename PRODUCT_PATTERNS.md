# Human Backstory — Product Patterns

## CANON product: YouTube thumbnail

Canvas: 1280×720, 16:9.

Core ingredients:
- one subject or symbolic object
- 2–4 word uppercase hook
- one meaningful symbolic device
- one red accent
- deep negative space
- grain / vignette
- monochrome or cold desaturated image treatment

Approved composition families:
- Layout A: copy left, subject right
- Layout B: subject left, copy right
- Layout C: symbolic/object-led composition using negative space

Do not repeat an identical construction on consecutive uploads.

## CANON product: Evidence label

Labels:
- FACT
- CONSENSUS
- HYPOTHESIS
- INFERENCE

Appearance:
- near-black container
- thin light border
- uppercase display typography
- white text
- red accent reserved for HYPOTHESIS / INFERENCE when useful

## CANON product: Editorial science frame

Use:
- near-black ground
- thin white lines
- restrained diagrams
- cold monochrome
- negative space
- grain and vignette

## PROVISIONAL products

Landing pages, presentation slides, dashboards, long-form web articles, and social card systems are extensions. Build them from the canon visual grammar, but label their new rules provisional until approved.
