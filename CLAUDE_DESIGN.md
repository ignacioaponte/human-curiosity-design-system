# Claude Design Instructions — Human Backstory

This is the primary AI-facing instruction set.

## Identity

Always call the brand **Human Backstory**.

Human Backstory is not a generic science brand, productivity brand, horror brand, or self-help brand. It combines evolutionary psychology, neuroscience, biology, human behavior, dark philosophy, and documentary visual language.

Target reaction:

> “I am seeing something uncomfortable and revealing about myself.”

Not physical danger. Not corporate education. Not glossy futurism.

## Canon vs extension

Before creating anything, classify every decision as either:

- **CANON** — explicitly supported by the approved brand identity, approved visual examples, or canon tokens.
- **PROVISIONAL / DESIGN SYSTEM EXTENSION** — useful but not yet specified by the brand.

Never silently convert a provisional choice into canon.

Do not invent as canon:
- grey ramps
- hover/pressed colors
- body fonts
- spacing scales
- type scales
- motion timings
- new symbolic devices
- landing-page patterns
- slide patterns

## Core visual grammar

A typical Human Backstory composition uses:
1. One human/biological subject OR one strong symbolic object.
2. One short verbal hook.
3. One meaningful symbolic device.
4. One red accent.
5. Deep negative space.
6. Grain and vignette.

Every element must carry meaning.

## Color — CANON

Use `tokens/colors.json`.

- Ground: `#0A0A0A`
- Vignette: `#060606`
- Text: `#F2F2F3`
- Accent: `#C0413F`

Red represents fracture, wound, unresolved tension, uncomfortable truth, biological or psychological rupture. It is structural, never decorative.

## Typography — CANON

Display typeface: **Barlow Condensed**, weight **900**.

Use uppercase for:
- wordmark
- thumbnail text
- evidence labels
- on-screen AI text when text is unavoidable

No body typeface is canon yet. If a product needs body copy, label the choice provisional.

## Photography / image generation

Default:
- B&W or near-monochrome cold duotone
- hard single-side lighting
- deep shadow
- high contrast
- subtle grain
- desaturated skin/environment
- introspective, tense, psychologically unresolved subject

Avoid:
- bright lifestyle imagery
- saturated color
- flat lighting
- glossy corporate portraits
- generic AI-future imagery
- neon cyberpunk
- office-stock language

## Thumbnail system — CANON

Canvas: 1280×720, 16:9.

Thumbnail text:
- normally 2–4 words
- uppercase
- names sensation, verdict, contradiction, or uncomfortable implication
- does not repeat the title mechanism

Layout families evidenced by the approved brand book:
- text left / subject right
- subject left / text right
- object-led or device-led composition using negative space

Alternate composition across consecutive uploads so the feed feels like a family, not a duplicated template.

## Symbolic devices

Use one main device per composition.

### Fracture
Pain, rupture, evolutionary tradeoff, bodily limitation, rejection, conflict.

### Open ring / incomplete loop
Rumination, unresolved memory, incomplete closure, repetition, psychological loops.

### Broken timeline
Missing memory, development, evolution, temporal discontinuity.

### Fragmentation
Rejection, social pain, identity damage, loss.

### Neural / constellation trace
Systems, cognition, relationships between mechanisms, explanatory science graphics.

## Evidence labels

Allowed:
- FACT
- CONSENSUS
- HYPOTHESIS
- INFERENCE

Use a black container with thin light border and white uppercase type. Red may be used for HYPOTHESIS/INFERENCE when useful, while respecting the one-accent principle.

## Interior video graphics

Quieter than thumbnails:
- fine lines
- minimal diagrams
- black fields
- cold monochrome
- long shadows
- restrained motion
- pauses and negative space

Avoid corporate iconography, gears, light bulbs, rockets, saturated infographics, and repetitive generic zoom-ins.

## Global finish

Unify stock and AI through the final grade:
- dark/cold grade
- strong desaturation
- vignette
- film grain

## Decision rule

When in doubt, remove an element.

Human Backstory should feel intentional, severe, intelligent, scientific, and psychologically charged.
